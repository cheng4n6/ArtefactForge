### Features

* **Case report:** each host shows when it was collected, its time zone and its last boot. The collection time comes from the collector (Velociraptor, UAC, a KAPE console log, or the run itself for a live system), and an estimate is marked Approx. Hover a time for where it came from.
* **Case report:** an address that is in the IP history links to it, and opens the lookup on that address.
* **Case report:** known addresses are labelled wherever they appear, such as DC or VPN. Domain controllers, DNS servers and gateways are labelled from the system summaries; add your own, single addresses or ranges, with Known addresses on the Analysis tab (KnownAddresses.csv in the case folder).
